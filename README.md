# 📊 Adidas Sales Analysis Dashboard | Power BI

# 📌 Project Overview
- This Power BI repository contains an end-to-end interactive Adidas Sales Analysis Dashboard. The project transforms raw multi-region retail sales data into key performance indicators (KPIs) and trend analyses to evaluate revenue generation, operating profit margins, top-performing sales channels/retailers, and regional product trends across the United States.

# 🔍 Key Insights & Visualizations

## 1. Monthly Sales Trends (Line/Area Chart)
- Dynamic sales trajectory throughout the year, peaking in July ($96M) and August ($92M), with a secondary end-of-year surge in December ($86M).
- Low sales periods observed during Q1, hitting a base in March ($57M).


## 2. Product-Wise Sales (Horizontal Bar Chart)
- Men's Street Footwear generated the highest sales volume at $209M, followed closely by Women's Apparel at $179M.


# Full product category breakdown:
- Men's Street Footwear: $209M
- Women's Apparel: $179M
- Men's Athletic Footwear: $154M
- Women's Street Footwear: $128M
- Men's Apparel: $124M
- Women's Athletic Footwear: $107M

## 3. Retailer-Wise Performance
- West Gear leads as the top retail partner with $243M in sales, followed by Foot Locker ($220M) and Sports Direct ($183M).
- E-commerce platforms such as Amazon ($78M) and traditional retail giants like Walmart ($75M) represent key growth segments.


## 4. Regional & State Distribution
- West Region accounts for the highest market share at 29.96%, followed by the Northeast (20.72%) and Southeast (18.13%).
- State-level heatmap displays strong revenue density in California, Texas, and Florida.

# 🚀 How to Run/View
- Download the .pbix file from this repository.
- Open it using Power BI Desktop.
- Use the top slicers to filter data dynamically by Sales Method (In-Store, Online, Outlet) or adjust the Date Range Slider.
